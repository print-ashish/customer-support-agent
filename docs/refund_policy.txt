REFUND POLICY

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. 

1. Eligibility for Refunds
To be eligible for a refund, you must request it within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and in the same condition that you received it.

2. Non-Refundable Items
Certain items cannot be refunded, including perishable goods, personalized items, and final sale merchandise.

3. Process
If your order is eligible for a refund, we will process the refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

4. Late or Missing Refunds
If you haven't received a refund yet, first check your bank account again. Then contact your credit card company, it may take some time before your refund is officially posted.
